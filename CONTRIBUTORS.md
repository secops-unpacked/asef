## ARMM foundations

ASEF draws on the action catalogue and human-involvement vocabulary of the [AI Response Maturity Model](https://armm.secops-unpacked.ai/), developed by **Andrei Cotaie, Valeriu-Cristian Miron and Filip Stojkovski**.

ASEF extends evaluation across the SecOps lifecycle and uses its own current needs-led approach. The present framework does not use Builder T/C/I sums or Explorer-to-Expert tiers as product rankings.
